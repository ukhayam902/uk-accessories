# UK Accessories — SEO keyword clusters (targets, not results)

> Internal document, not a user-facing page. Every keyword below is a
> TARGET for titles, descriptions, headings and page copy — not a ranking
> result. No search volumes, traffic figures or ranking positions are
> claimed here, and no keyword is guaranteed to rank on top of Google.
> Rankings depend on Google's index, competition, content quality and
> many factors outside this site's control. Verify real performance only
> in Google Search Console after the site is verified and indexed.

## Scope: SEO + GEO + AEO

- **SEO (traditional search):** crawlable pages, canonicals, sitemap,
  robots.txt, titles/descriptions, Product/Organization/WebSite schema,
  internal links (Shop, product pages, FAQ, About).
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** llms.txt / llms-full.txt,
  citable fact-first copy, schema, so ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and
  Google AI Overviews can cite the store accurately. Note: llms.txt
  alone does not affect Google Search ranking.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** concise, direct-answer content
  for real customer questions — short answer first, then detail — on
  FAQ and About, and as FAQPage schema, so answer engines can quote a
  direct answer.

## Rules applied

- Confirmed facts only. No invented opening hours, geo coordinates,
  social links, business email, reviews, ratings or Google Business
  Profile.
- Prices are sample prices — pages and llms.txt say so, and tell
  customers to confirm on WhatsApp before ordering.
- International delivery is never claimed as available.
- No keyword stuffing: keywords appear naturally in titles,
  descriptions and headings, once each where they fit.
- The 7 deleted products (p9–p15: speaker, stand, 65W adapter, finger
  sleeves, car holder, braided cable, mini power bank) are NOT targeted
  and are NOT in the sitemap or Product schema.

## What was NOT done, and why

- No doorway/location pages were created (the claude-seo sitemap
  skill warns against city-name-swapped pages with no unique content).
- No sitemap index was created: the site has 17 sitemap URLs, far
  below the 50,000 URL / 50 MB threshold.
- No `<lastmod>` values were added to the sitemap: lastmod must be
  verifiably accurate, and uniform dates would be misleading.
- No aggregateRating / review schema: no reviews exist, and none were
  invented.
- No WebMCP tools, no ai-catalog.json, no Content-Signal training
  policy: those are optional/draft agentic items and the training
  policy was not decided by the owner. robots.txt allows AI search
  crawlers explicitly; that is separate from any training opt-out.
- No ranking guarantee is made for any keyword in this document.
